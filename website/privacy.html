Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vagai Silambam mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares information. The app is used by Silambam instructors and academy administrators to manage students, attendance, fees and related records.

1. Information the app handles

Records you enter about your academy. Instructors and administrators may enter:

  • Student details: name (English and Tamil), student ID, photo, date of birth, gender, contact number, address, joining date, training level, training centre, previous experience and medical notes
  • Guardian and emergency contact details: name, relationship, phone numbers, email and address
  • Attendance records, fees and payments, uniform issues, events and registrations, achievements and certificates
  • Staff accounts: name, username, password, email, phone and role

Information from your Google account (optional). If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google account email address, name and profile picture, and permission to access Google Drive files created by the app. We request only these permissions:

  • userinfo.email and userinfo.profile: to show which account is connected
  • drive.file: to create, update and read only the files and folders the app itself creates in your Google Drive. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Device permissions. The app asks for camera and photo access only when you choose to take or pick a student photo, and file access only when you import or export a file.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or usage analytics, and it contains no advertising or tracking software.

2. How the information is used

  • To register students and run the academy: attendance, fees, events, certificates and reports
  • To back up your records and save exported reports and photos to your own Google Drive, when you connect Google
  • To send attendance to a Google Sheet that you set up and control, when you enable that feature

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to managing your academy.

3. Where the data is stored

All records are stored in a database on your device. We do not operate our own servers and we do not receive a copy of your data. If you connect Google, backups and exports are stored in your own Google Drive account and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Data leaves your device only when you ask it to: backing up or exporting to your Google Drive, sending attendance to your own Google Sheet, or sharing a report file using your phone's share menu.

5. Google API Services – Limited Use

Vagai Silambam's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and export features described above, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is not read by humans except with your explicit consent or where required by law.

6. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access, and staff passwords never leave the device. Connections to Google use encrypted HTTPS. Please protect your phone with a screen lock, because anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to open the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Records stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Files the app created in Google Drive stay there until you delete them from Google Drive.
  • You can disconnect Google at any time by signing out in the app, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Because we do not hold your data on our servers, we cannot delete it for you, but we are happy to help. Contact us at the email below.

8. Children's information

The app is intended for adult instructors and administrators, not for children. Academies may record information about students who are under 18. The academy using the app is responsible for obtaining consent from students' parents or guardians before entering their information, and for keeping it accurate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will show when it was last updated.
