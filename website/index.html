Vagai Silambam

Attendance and student management for Silambam martial arts academies.

What the app does

Vagai Silambam is an Android app for Silambam instructors and academy administrators. It replaces paper registers with a simple digital registry that works on the instructor's phone.

  • Register students with their details, photo, guardian and emergency contacts
  • Take daily attendance and view attendance history
  • Track fees and payments, uniforms, events, achievements and certificates
  • Generate reports and export attendance as CSV files
  • Available in English and Tamil

Google account and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. If you connect your Google account, Vagai Silambam uses it only to back up your academy's records and export reports to your own Google Drive. The app can only see and change the Drive files it creates itself; it cannot read your other Drive files.

Your data stays with you

All records are stored on your device. We do not run our own servers, we do not sell data, and we do not show ads. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.
