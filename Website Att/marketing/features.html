Complete SaaS Architecture

Built around how a Silambam academy runs

Not a generic school ERP with labels swapped. No classes, no subjects, no exam marks — just pure martial arts training, gradings, weapons, and tournaments.

  • Student Master Registry

    Student ID and roll numbers, dual English & தமிழ் names, photo avatars, date of birth, emergency contacts, joining date, training rank sash, dojo branch, instructor, and medical notes.

  • Session Training Attendance

    Recorded per training session rather than school day. Mark 50 students present in one click, then modify exceptions: Present, Absent, Late, and Leave with custom remarks.

  • Retention & Attendance Insights

    Real-time attendance percentage per student, monthly summaries, absent lists, and an automated low-attendance report so no martial artist quietly drifts away.

  • Tournaments & Form Demonstrations

    District, state and national level championships, intensive weapon camps, and public demonstrations. Track registration deadlines, participant brackets, fees, and bout results.

  • Medals & Belt Achievements

    Winner, Runner-up, Third Place and Participation honors — with permanent medal tallies and belt promotion dates stored against each student's profile.

  • Fee Ledger & Digital Receipts

    Monthly training, admission, event, camp, uniform and certificate fees. Track paid, pending, overdue, partial and waived dues with automated digital PDF receipts.

  • Uniforms & Weapon Inventory

    Kurta & dhoti sets, dojo t-shirts, track pants, grading sashes, and bamboo staffs (Nedunkambu) — tracked by size, quantity, payment status, and condition.

  • Grading Certificates

    Issue belt grading and tournament achievement certificates, attach them to the student record, and automatically archive copies in your own Google Drive.

  • Exportable Audits & Reports

    Master student rosters, session registers, monthly fee collections, pending dues, tournament participation logs — exported to CSV, Excel, or PDF.

Daily Workflow

How Vagai Silambam fits your day

  • Phone at the dojo, browser at the desk

    Instructors take attendance on Android, including with zero signal. Office staff manage billing and reports on a laptop. Both sync seamlessly.

  • Your Google account, not ours

    Connect your own Google account once. Photos and certificates go to your Drive; attendance is written to your Sheet. You keep access even if you leave.

  • English and தமிழ்

    Switch language from any screen. Student names are stored in both scripts, so records read naturally either way.

  • Multiple branches

    Run several training centres under one academy, with per-branch students and reporting on the higher plans.

  • Roles that make sense

    Admin, instructor and accounts staff each see what they need. Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

  • Separated by design

    Every academy's data is isolated. One academy can never read another's students, fees or attendance.
