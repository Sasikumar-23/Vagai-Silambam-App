Built around how a Silambam academy runs

Not a school ERP with the words swapped. No classes, no subjects, no exam marks — just training.

👥

Students

Student ID and roll number, names in English and Tamil, photo, date of birth, guardians and emergency contacts, joining date, training level, branch, instructor, medical notes and previous experience.

✓

Training attendance

Per session, not per school day. Mark all present in one tap, then change the exceptions. Present, absent, late and leave, each with remarks.

📈

Attendance insight

Attendance percentage per student, daily and monthly views, absent and late lists, and a low-attendance report so nobody quietly drifts away.

★

Events

Competitions at district, state and national level, training camps, demonstrations, workshops and tournaments. Registration deadlines, participant limits, fees and results.

🏅

Results & achievements

Winner, runner-up, third place or participated — with medals, certificates and a permanent record on each student's profile.

₹

Fees

Monthly training, admission, event, competition, camp, uniform and certificate fees. Paid, pending, overdue, partial and waived, with payment history and receipts.

👕

Uniforms & equipment

Kurta and dhoti, academy t-shirts, track pants, grading sashes and bamboo staffs — by size and quantity, with payment status, condition and return dates.

📜

Certificates

Issue grading and tournament certificates, keep them against the student, and store copies in your own Google Drive.

📊

Reports

Student lists, attendance, fee collection, pending and overdue fees, event participation and results, uniform collection — exported to CSV, Excel or PDF.

How it fits your day

📱

Phone at the dojo, browser at the desk

Instructors take attendance on Android, including with no signal. Office staff work on a laptop. Both see the same academy.

☁️

Your Google account, not ours

Connect your own Google account once. Photos and certificates go to your Drive; attendance is written to your Sheet. You keep access even if you leave.

அ

English and தமிழ்

Switch language from any screen. Student names are stored in both scripts, so records read naturally either way.

🏢

Multiple branches

Run several training centres under one academy, with per-branch students and reporting on the higher plans.

👤

Roles that make sense

Admin, instructor and accounts staff each see what they need. Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

🔒

Separated by design

Every academy's data is isolated. One academy can never read another's students, fees or attendance.
