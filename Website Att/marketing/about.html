About Vagai Silambam

Silambam is one of the oldest martial arts in Tamil Nadu. The admin around it should not be the hard part.

Why it exists

Most academies run on paper registers and a spreadsheet someone keeps on their phone. Attendance gets copied twice, fees go uncollected because nobody noticed, and a student's grading history lives in a WhatsApp thread. The software built for schools does not fit: there are no classes, subjects or exam marks here — there are batches, gradings and tournaments.

How it was built

It started as an offline Android app for one academy, used at real training sessions before anything was sold. Features arrived because an instructor needed them that week — marking a full batch present in one tap, bilingual student names, tracking who still owes this month's fee. The web version came later, for the people doing the paperwork at a desk.

What we care about

Your records belong to you: photos and certificates go to your own Google Drive, attendance to your own Sheet, and you can export everything at any time. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not delete an academy's history because a subscription lapsed.
