Questions, answered simply

How much does it cost?

Plans start at ₹499 a month. You can try everything free for 14 days first.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. You can start the trial without a card or any payment.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Mark attendance and fees with no signal. It updates by itself when the internet returns. Connect to the internet at least once every 3 days.

Is my data safe?

Your attendance goes to your own Google Sheet and your files to your own Drive. Each academy sees only its own information.

Can I bring my student list from Excel?

Yes. Send us your Excel list on WhatsApp and we will help you add it.

Is it available in Tamil?

Yes. The app and this website work in English and Tamil. Switch any time.

How do my masters and staff log in?

You add each person with a username and password on the website. You can remove their access any time.

What if I stop paying?

Your data is kept safe. Renew any time to continue using the app.
