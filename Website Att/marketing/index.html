Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trials, offline functionality, Google integrations, and data privacy.

Do I need a credit card for the 14-day free trial?

No. You can create your academy account and start exploring all features immediately. We never ask for payment details until you decide to subscribe to a plan.

Does attendance tracking work completely offline?

Yes. The native Android app is engineered specifically for open-ground training camps and dojos with zero signal. Records are saved locally in SQLite and automatically sync with the cloud database once you regain internet access.

Where is our academy data stored?

Your tenant database is securely hosted in encrypted PostgreSQL. Photos, certificates, and student documents go directly to your personal Google Drive, and attendance logs are appended to your own Google Sheet. You retain full ownership even if you ever cancel.

Can other academies see our student or fee records?

Absolutely not. Vagai Silambam enforces strict tenant isolation at both the application router and database layer. No academy can ever access another dojo's students, financial transactions, or reports.

Can we switch the interface between English and தமிழ்?

Yes! Every screen in both the web portal and Android app supports instant bilingual switching. Student names are also natively stored in both English and தமிழ் scripts.

Can we import our existing student Excel or paper registers?

Yes. You can import existing spreadsheets via CSV in Settings, or backup files from earlier versions of the Vagai Silambam Android app. For larger dojos, our team assists with full data onboarding.
